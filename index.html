Discovery Brief · For Paul Husband

Tell us the story of your business.

In your own words, straight into your iPhone. Take us through it however feels natural — the more detail you give us, the better.

A short brief from the team at Digital Movement UK
Before you start

In your own words.

The better our team understands your business, the stronger the work. So before anything else, talk us through it — generously and specifically. The detail is what makes it valuable.

How to record it — on your iPhone
  • Open the Voice Memos app (the grey icon that comes with your iPhone).
  • Tap the red circle. Say the section number, then just talk through the questions.
  • Be specific — real names, places, numbers, short stories.
  • Don't polish it. Rambling is welcome; we'll shape it.
  • One long memo, or one per section. Then send it over to us.
The single best thing you can do: be concrete. "We lifted footfall at X by Y%" beats "we improve performance." Names and numbers are gold.
The brief

Nine short sections.

Each has a goal and a few questions to answer out loud. Roughly 15–20 minutes if you talk freely.

01
Positioning

Who you are & what you stand for

Goal: nail the one thing Husband Retail is, in your words.
  • In a sentence or two, what is Husband Retail and what do you do?
  • What do you genuinely do better than anyone else in retail real estate?
  • If a developer described you to a colleague, what would you want them to say?
  • What do you believe about great retail that others don't?
  • Why does the work matter — to your clients, to a city, to you?
02
Services

What you do — and what clients receive

Goal: make every service concrete and tangible.
  • Walk me through your core services, one by one.
  • For each one, what does the client actually get at the end?The real deliverable — a report, a strategy, a let scheme, a signed deal.
  • What does a typical engagement look like, from first call to finish?
  • Which service is most in demand right now? Which is most strategic for you?
  • Your newer direction — asset management & investment advisory — what does it include?
03
Audience

Who you do it for — and want more of

Goal: be clear on exactly who you serve.
  • Who are your clients — developers, investors, retailers, landlords? Describe the main types.
  • Who is your ideal client — the kind of project you'd happily take ten more of?
  • What problem are they usually trying to solve when they come to you?
  • What worries or hesitations do they have before hiring an advisor?
  • Who do you not want to attract?
04
Markets

Where you operate

Goal: map your footprint and your home turf.
  • Which markets and cities are you active in today?
  • Where is your core / home market, and where are you strongest?
  • Any market you've worked in that surprises people?
  • How do you actually work across markets — local partners, on the ground, networks?
05
Proof

Track record & results

Goal: gather the hard proof — names, numbers, outcomes.
  • The headline numbers — how many projects, how many years, how many markets?
  • The names you're proud to be associated with — clients, brands, schemes?
  • A result you're proud of — what changed because of your work?Footfall, rents, lettings, sales, value, a scheme that wouldn't have happened.
  • Any awards, the book, press or recognition worth featuring?
  • Would any clients give a short testimonial — and who should we ask?
06
Case studies

Signature projects — tell the stories

Goal: three to five flagship projects, told as stories.
  • Pick a few signature projects. For each — what was it, and where?
  • What was the brief, or the problem the client had?
  • What did you specifically do?
  • What was the result — ideally a number or a memorable outcome?
  • Why does this one matter — what does it prove about you?
Repeat these five for each project.
07
Growth

Where you're going next

Goal: capture the forward story and your business-development goals.
  • Which markets or regions do you want to grow into next?
  • Which services do you want more of going forward?
  • What kind of clients or projects are you actively trying to win?
  • What's the one-to-three-year ambition for the business?
  • Is there anyone in particular you'd love us to help you reach?
08
Look, feel & voice

How it should look and sound

Goal: capture the look, tone and colours that feel right for Husband Retail.
  • How should it feel to someone landing on it — in three or four words?e.g. premium, understated, confident, warm.
  • Are there websites, brands or magazines whose look and feel you admire? What is it about them?
  • How should the writing sound — more formal and understated, or warm and conversational?
  • Any words or a tone you'd want associated with Husband Retail — and any to avoid?
  • Any colours that feel like "you", or that you'd want to steer clear of?
  • Do you lean toward classic black-and-white restraint, or something with more colour?
09
The goal

What "success" looks like

Goal: tell us what a great outcome looks like for you.
  • When the new site is live, what should it do for the business?
  • What single action do you want a great-fit visitor to take?
  • A year from now, if the site had been a brilliant investment — what happened?
  • Any websites — yours or others' — you admire? What about them?
  • Anything you definitely want, or definitely want to avoid?
When you're done

Send it over — we'll take it from there.

That's everything. The more generous and specific you are, the better — every name, number and story gives our team something to work with. Record it whenever suits you, then send the memo (or a transcript) over to us. Thank you, Paul.
